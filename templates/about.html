About LokBandhu

Bridging the gap between administration and citizens through transparent digital governance, real-time civic updates, and automated grievance workflows.

Our Core Mission

LokBandhu was built to decentralize public grievance monitoring and streamline civic updates across regional tiers. By providing clear portals tailored for Rural, Urban, and Metropolitan regions, we ensure every citizen, regardless of geography, has an equal voice in community development.

Mini Project Genesis

The concept of LokBandhu was originally formulated and engineered as an academic Mini Project at DY Patil.

Developed & Conceptualized by:
Shreeya Parab, Saniya Rajguru, and Pratiksha Navle.
